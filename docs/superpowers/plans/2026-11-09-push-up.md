# Push up from below (Subsurface phase 3) — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** What lives below the wallpaper reaches up through it while the opening is shut: news pushes a soft
bulge and its words up through the picture, a playing thing glows through, a visualiser moves the wallpaper,
the clock sweeps a light at the hour, and a new notice pings a spark whose light travels to the app's window
and traces its border.

**Architecture:** Pure decisions in `shell/lib/surface/skin.luau` (which widget, its words, the spark's spot, the
app's window, the queue). A logic module `shell/lib/surface/light.luau` (`return function(ctx)`, like
`lib/wbelow_feed`) runs them from mooi-surface's existing watchers (media, notices, clock, window) and writes the
facts. Two new surfaces generated by `tools/gen-surface.py`: `skin` (level bottom, over the wallpaper, patches
only) and `light` (level overlay, click-through but for the spark). Two shaders: `bulge.wgsl`, `skin.wgsl`.

**Tech Stack:** Pleamar scenes (language 0.9), Luau logic, WGSL shaders, Python generators, Lua 5.4 unit tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-subsurface-design.md` §9, §9a (as revised by
`2026-11-09-widgets-below-design.md`: the things below are widgets; "above" is a widget kept above).

## Global Constraints

- `meet` per widget below, an option of four kinds: nowplaying `woven`, notices `woven`, visualiser `moves`,
  clock `moves`; each may be `still`. A widget kept above is "above" (no option).
- Woven: a song change (Now Playing) or a new notice (Notices) bulges the wallpaper over the widget's place for a
  few hundred ms and its words press up, then settle back (≈ 4 s in all). High contrast (`theme.contrast` 1): a
  solid plate under the words.
- Moves: a playing visualiser ripples the wallpaper round its place with the music (`lv.level`); the clock sweeps a
  slow light over its place at the hour. Always a patch round the thing, never the whole screen.
- Glow through (`surface.glow` off / subtle / bright, existing): at rest, a faint breathing glow at Now Playing while
  it plays and at Notices while it holds news.
- The ping (`surface.ping`, existing): a spark at the Notices widget's place, or — when a window covers it — at the
  nearest screen edge; then (`surface.trace`, new, default on) a line to the window of the app that sent it (its
  class or title matched; the focused window when none matches) and once round its border, then fades. A click on
  the spark opens the Nexus (the notices). At most one at a time; a burst becomes one spark with a count.
- Reduced motion (`motion.reduced`): the spark and the border lit at once, no travel; the words without the bulge.
- Nothing while the opening is open (it shows the real things), while a window is fullscreen, or with Surface off.
  `surface.push` (new, default on) turns the bulge and words off.
- New settings keys are appended after the last setter slot (no earlier control's number moves).
- Contour traces need `wall-sense.json`, which does not exist yet: the spec's fallback, the glow alone.

## Review Focus

- A burst of notices (five in a second): one spark with a count, not five overlapping lights — Task 1 queue test.
- A notice from an app with no window, or no window at all: the line goes to the focused window, or only the spark
  shows — Task 1 `window_for` test.
- The Notices widget kept above or `still`, or no Notices widget: no bulge; the spark falls back to the screen's top
  right edge — Task 1.
- The opening opened mid-push: the skin hides at once (`not op_vis` in its `open:`) — Task 3 scene test.
- A fullscreen window: no skin, no light — Task 2 logic test.

### Task 1: The pure decisions — lib/surface/skin, and `meet`

**Files:** Create `shell/lib/surface/skin.luau`; Modify `shell/lib/widgets.luau` (OPTIONS `meet` for the four kinds,
`M.option_string` accepts `woven`/`moves`/`still`); Test `tests/unit/run.lua` (section "push up from below (D217)").

**Interfaces — Produces:**
- `skin.MEET` = `{ nowplaying = "woven", notices = "woven", visualiser = "moves", clock = "moves" }`.
- `skin.meet(w) -> string` (the option, else the kind's default, else `"still"`; `"above"` for a widget kept above).
- `skin.target(widgets, kind, want, bar_h) -> {x, y, w, h} | nil` — the first widget of `kind` below whose meet is `want`, in screen coordinates.
- `skin.words(kind, data) -> string` — nowplaying: `"title — artist"`; notices: `"app · title"`; at most 60 characters (an ellipsis).
- `skin.queue() -> q`; `skin.push(q, item) -> boolean start` (busy: merged into the waiting one, `n` counted); `skin.done(q) -> item | nil` (the next).
- `skin.covered(pt, rects) -> boolean`; `skin.spark(box, rects, scr) -> {x, y}` (the box's centre, or the nearest screen edge point 14 px in when covered; no box: the top right corner).
- `skin.window_for(app, clients, ws, focused) -> {x, y, w, h} | nil` and `skin.rects(clients, ws, mon) -> {…}` (mapped, not hidden, on workspace `ws`, minus the monitor's origin); `skin.fullscreen(clients, ws) -> boolean`.
- `skin.edge_point(from, rect) -> {x, y}` (where the line meets the window's border).
- `skin.hour(prev, now) -> boolean` ("HH:MM" strings; true when the hour changed).

- [ ] Step 1: tests for each (including the Review Focus cases), Step 2: watch them fail, Step 3: implement, Step 4: `lua5.4 tests/unit/run.lua` all pass, Step 5: commit.

**Interfaces — Consumes:** Task 1. **Produces (facts the scene reads):** `pu_on, pu_tk` (bulge target 0/1),
`pu_x, pu_y, pu_w, pu_h`, text `pu_words`, `pu_plate`; `sk_on`, `sk_mx, sk_my, sk_mp` (Now Playing, playing),
`sk_nx, sk_ny, sk_nn` (Notices, news), `sk_vx, sk_vy, sk_vw, sk_vh, sk_vm` (a visualiser that moves, playing),
`sk_cx, sk_cy, sk_cw, sk_ch, sk_sw_t` (the clock and its sweep target); `lt_on, lt_sx, lt_sy, lt_tx, lt_ty,
lt_rx, lt_ry, lt_rw, lt_rh, lt_n, lt_st, lt_tt, lt_bt, lt_kt` (spark, travel, border, fade targets); event `lt_go`
(the spark pressed → `mooi nexus`).
- Fullscreen and the windows: `hyprctl -j clients` + `-j monitors` read when a light or push starts (rare), and the
  active window's fullscreen on window events, at most once a second.
- [ ] Steps: tests on the module's strings (guards: Surface on, opening shut, not fullscreen, the settings; one
  watcher per service — the module watches nothing itself); implement; `luac5.4 -p`; tests pass; commit.

### Task 3: The scene — `skin` and `light`, two shaders

**Files:** Modify `tools/gen-surface.py` (+ regenerate `shell/mooi-surface.plm`); Create `shell/shaders/bulge.wgsl`,
`shell/shaders/skin.wgsl`; Test `tests/unit/run.lua`.
- `skin`: level bottom, `open: sf_on and not op_vis and (pu_on or sk_on)`, `rate: 30, 20 while calm`; the glow
  patches (440 px round each point), the visualiser's patch (its box + 80 px) and the clock's sweep patch, each a
  group with a clip box and `shader: skin, …` over `rv_img`; the bulge patch (`shader: bulge`) and the words.
- `light`: level overlay, `open: lt_on`, `keyboard: none`; the spark (with its count), the travelling line (a comet:
  from `mix(s, t, max(0, lt_t - 0.3))` to `mix(s, t, lt_t)`), the border traced side by side by `lt_b`, all faded by
  `lt_k`; one zone, the spark's.
- [ ] Steps: tests asserting the surfaces, their `open:` guards, the one zone, the shaders' files; regenerate;
  `pleamar --check`; commit.
