# The Pill, round two, stage 2: many pills, connections, motion — plan

> For agentic workers: superpowers:executing-plans. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-pill-round-two-design.md
> (D171) §3, §4, plus the owner's asks of 2026-11-07: **several pills placed wherever they want** (not only
> left · centre · right) and a Pill that **moves and morphs organically** — "like Venom does".

## Global Constraints
- Setter codes append-only (lib/setters.luau ↔ tools/gen-settings.py); never hand-edit `<gen:edit>`.
- A fact the logic writes that another reader follows goes through `opens.*` / a callback (`on("fact:X")` is
  not heard for the logic's own writes, D170, D171).
- mooi.luau's main chunk is at 200 locals: new state goes in tables. bar.luau keeps to one local per pill's state,
  not one set of locals per pill.
- One chassis piece per pill (`geo.pills`), never re-derived in the scene; the frame draws what the bar places.
- Every motion on the material's springs, `motion.reduced` / the saver honoured; nothing turns while idle.
- Live tests restore settings byte for byte, the players and the volume; never drive the real pointer.

## Review Focus
1. A hand-edited `pill.slots` with a bad x/anchor, a duplicate, or a slot past the screen edge: the Pill falls
   back to its default place and is still reachable (never off-screen, never 0-wide while shown).
2. Two pills with the same views and one activity: each shows it; a press on either acts on it, not on both.
3. A vanished pill's seam and its neighbours: pointing at one seam must not wake the other.
4. The board's lip and the record: with `shape = record` the board still unfolds from the disc's place.
5. Music stopping while a record turns: the disc settles (springs), stops, and the next song re-reads the cover.
6. Reduced motion and Lite: the morph and the node that shoots across do not run; the state still changes.

- [ ] The capsule breathing while it waits (D173's pointer warmth carries it; nothing when hidden).
- [ ] **Many closing at once** (moons expiring, the board folding with docked chips): each part dims in

- [ ] **BMO**: the game-over (8-bit blink, "GAME OVER") when a recording or a timer ends, and a power-down (eyes
      narrowing to a line, then off) when his pill tucks away.
- [ ] **The morph** (the owner's ask, 2026-11-07): a new `pill.shape = morph` where the silhouette is drawn from

      pill. Bounded to one spring per point (no per-frame simulation), and off under reduced motion (a plain
      capsule, the state changes only). Written as `lib/pillmorph.luau` (pure control points) + a component, so
      its shapes can be tested without the scene.
- [ ] Tests: links and the transfer's arithmetic; the morph's points at rest and at each waypoint; source checks
